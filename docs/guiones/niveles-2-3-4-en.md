# Levels 2, 3 and 4 — narrated scripts (English)

Translation of `niveles-2-3-4-borrador.md` (v2, with Reyna's corrections of Nov 14). Same parts, same order and the same silence after each one.
Only the text under each heading is read aloud. Target 16 minutes, minimum 15. Natural pace.

# Level 2 · Inner Calm — Stillness and silence

## 01 Welcome (silence 8 s)
Welcome to Inner Calm, the second level of Súper Conciencia. In Awakening you learned to notice and to come back. Today we are going to explore small moments of stillness, even if there is noise around you or a lot still going on inside. You don't need to feel calm to do this practice. You can begin exactly as you are. As always, you can pause or stop whenever you need to.

## 02 Arriving (silence 15 s)
Settle into a posture you can hold without effort. Sitting, lying down, or however your body asks for today. If you like, close your eyes; if not, let your gaze rest low and soft. Feel the support beneath you. You don't have to hold yourself up alone: the floor, the chair or the bed is holding you right now.

## 03 What you bring (silence 20 s)
Before we go on, notice how you are arriving. Maybe you come in a hurry, tired, or with something going around in your head. You don't need to hide it. You can say to yourself, inwardly: this is how I am today. Recognizing it is already a way of keeping yourself company. It doesn't have to disappear for you to begin.

## 04 Keeping the breath company (silence 30 s)
Bring your attention to your breath, just as it is. Don't make it slower or deeper. Simply observe it. Sometimes, when we watch it patiently, it settles on its own; other times it doesn't change, and that is fine too. Your only task is to keep it company.

## 05 The out-breath (silence 35 s)
Now pay attention to the moment the air leaves, that instant when the body lets go. You may notice your shoulders resting a little, your chest or belly softening. If you don't notice it, that's all right. Each time the air goes out, you can allow some part of you to rest as well. Without pushing. Just allowing.

## 06 The support (silence 35 s)
Let the breath follow its own course, without looking for pauses or changing its rhythm. Notice the contact of your body with the place that holds you. You can rest your attention on that support, while the air comes in and goes out on its own. There is nothing to reach for in this moment.

## 07 Listening to silence (silence 70 s)
Now open your attention to sounds. Listen to what is nearby, and then to what is farther away. And between one sound and the next, notice the silence around them. Sounds come and go; the silence stays in the background. I'll stay quiet while you listen.

## 08 When something feels restless (silence 25 s)
When you stay still, some restlessness may appear: the urge to move, to check your phone, to think about what's pending. That's normal. Stillness can feel uncomfortable at first. Don't fight that restlessness. Observe it with curiosity, the way you would watch the weather. And if you need to move, do it slowly, with attention, and then settle again.

## 09 The pause (silence 70 s)
Let's practice something that will help you during your day: the pause. When you notice a thought pulling at you, don't follow it right away. Feel one full breath, however it comes. Only then decide whether you want to follow it or let it go. Between what happens and what you do, there can be a small space. Practice it now, in silence.

## 10 The lake (silence 30 s)
If you like, imagine a lake in a peaceful place. When the wind blows, the surface ripples; farther down, the water moves less. Your thoughts and emotions can be like that wind on the surface. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep feeling your hands or listening to the sounds.

## 11 The turquoise sphere (silence 40 s)
If it helps, now imagine a sphere of soft turquoise light, floating near your chest. It doesn't do anything. It doesn't ask anything of you. It's simply there. Each time the air goes out, you can imagine its light spreading a little through your body, at its own pace. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep feeling your hands or listening to the sounds.

## 12 Calm (silence 90 s)
With each breath, you can silently repeat one word. As the air goes out: calm. Just that. Calm. Not as a command, but as an invitation. If it doesn't come today, the invitation stays open. Let that word keep you company during the silence.

## 13 Stillness (silence 160 s)
Now let go of the word as well. Let go of the image. Stay in stillness, without doing anything in particular. If the mind moves, let it move, and come back to the support of your body. I'm going to stay in silence with you for a long while, and I'll speak to you again when it's time to return.

## 14 Integration (silence 25 s)
Little by little, feel your whole body again and the place where you are. Notice how you feel now, without demanding a change. Perhaps you find some calm; perhaps you still feel restless. Either way, you have spent this time observing yourself and keeping yourself company.

## 15 Closing (silence 10 s)
Gently move your hands and feet. Stretch if you'd like, and open your eyes when you feel ready. During your day, when something unsettles you, remember the pause: one full breath before you respond. Thank you for giving yourself this time.

# Level 3 · Presence — Mindful presence

## 01 Welcome (silence 8 s)
Welcome to Presence, the third level of Súper Conciencia. You already know how to come back to the breath and how to pause. Today we are going to bring that attention to what you are living right now: your body, the sounds, the sensations. Being present isn't something special or far away. It is truly being where you already are.

## 02 Posture (silence 15 s)
Choose a posture that is comfortable and awake at the same time. Let your back be supported or upright without stiffness, and let your hands rest. You can close your eyes or keep a soft gaze. Take a moment to find your place.

## 03 Anchor (silence 30 s)
Let's begin with a familiar anchor: the breath. Feel it wherever you notice it most clearly, without changing it. If today you'd rather not focus on the breath, you can use the contact of your hands or your feet as your anchor. You choose your anchor.

## 04 Feet and floor (silence 30 s)
Now bring your attention to your feet. Feel their weight, their temperature, their contact with the floor or the surface where they rest. You may notice tingling, pressure, or almost nothing. All of it is valid. You're not looking for the right sensation. You're simply noticing what is there.

## 05a Body scan: feet and ankles (silence 5 s)
We're going to move through the body slowly, like a soft light. Notice your feet and ankles.

## 05b Body scan: legs and hips (silence 5 s)
Now bring your attention to your legs and hips.

## 05c Body scan: back and belly (silence 5 s)
Notice the contact of your back and the sensations in your belly.

## 05d Body scan: chest and shoulders (silence 5 s)
Feel your chest and your shoulders.

## 05e Body scan: arms and hands (silence 5 s)
Let your attention move down your arms, all the way to your hands.

## 05f Body scan: neck, face and head (silence 20 s)
Finally, your neck, your face and your head. You don't have to relax anything. Just greet each part with attention.

## 06 If there is discomfort (silence 25 s)
If you find tension or discomfort somewhere, you can stay there for a moment with kindness, or bring your attention to an area that feels neutral, like your hands. You decide. If something feels very uncomfortable, open your eyes or stop the practice. Taking care of yourself is also being present.

## 07 Listening (silence 70 s)
Now let the body move into the background and open your attention to sounds. Don't look for pleasant sounds. Listen to what is there: an engine, a voice, the wind, your own breathing. Notice how each sound begins, lasts a moment, and fades. You don't need to name it. I'll stay quiet while you listen.

## 08 The touch of the air (silence 30 s)
Now feel the air on your skin: on your face, your hands, your arms. Maybe it's cool, maybe warm. Maybe there's a very slight movement, or maybe you notice nothing at all. These are details that almost always go unnoticed. Presence begins in these small details.

## 09 Seeing what passes through the mind (silence 70 s)
Thoughts are also part of what is happening. When one appears, you can notice it the way you notice a sound: it appears, stays a moment, and goes. If it carries you away, don't scold yourself. Notice, and return to your anchor. Practice it in silence.

## 10 Your thoughts are not orders (silence 30 s)
Sometimes the mind says things like: you have to get up, you have to solve this now. Remember that a thought is not an order. You can listen to it, recognize that it wants to help you, and decide not to follow it for now. You are not every thought that passes. You are the one who notices them.

## 11 The clear light (silence 40 s)
If it helps, imagine a clear, soft light, like moonlight, illuminating the space where you are. It doesn't change anything; it simply lets you see what is there more clearly. That is what attention is like: it doesn't push, it doesn't judge. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep feeling your hands or listening to the sounds.

## 12 Here, now (silence 90 s)
With each breath, you can silently repeat. As the air comes in: here. As the air goes out: now. Here... now. When the mind travels to the past or the future, these two words bring you back. Let them keep you company in the silence.

## 13 Open presence (silence 160 s)
Now let go of the anchor and the words. Leave your attention open, without choosing anything in particular: sounds, sensations, thoughts, everything can appear and pass. You are simply present. If you get lost, return to your anchor for a moment. I'm going to stay in silence with you for a long while, and I'll speak to you again when it's time to return.

## 14 Integration (silence 25 s)
Little by little, feel your whole body again. Listen to the sounds of the place. Notice how you are now, without demanding a change. You can carry the attention you practiced into whatever you do next: washing a dish, walking, listening to someone.

## 15 Closing (silence 10 s)
Gently move your fingers and stretch if you'd like. Open your eyes when you feel ready. Today, choose one simple activity in your day and do it with your full attention, even for a minute. That is meditating too. Thank you for being present.

# Level 4 · The Heart — Compassion and gratitude

## 01 Welcome (silence 8 s)
Welcome to The Heart, the fourth level of Súper Conciencia. You have already learned to come back, to pause and to be present. Today we are going to bring that same attention to the way you treat yourself and others. We won't force any feeling. We'll simply open a little space for kindness.

## 02 Posture (silence 15 s)
Find a comfortable posture. If it helps, you can place one hand on your chest or on your belly, as a gesture of care. If you'd rather not, let your hands rest wherever they feel best. Close your eyes or keep a soft gaze.

## 03 Arriving in the body (silence 25 s)
Feel a few breaths, just as they come. Notice the contact with the place where you are. If you placed a hand on your chest, you can feel its warmth and the movement of the breath beneath it.

## 04 How you speak to yourself (silence 35 s)
Think for a moment about how you speak to yourself when something goes wrong. Often we are harder on ourselves than on anyone else. Don't judge yourself for that. Just notice it. And gently ask yourself: how would I speak to someone I love, if they were in my place?

## 05 Kind words for you (silence 70 s)
Let's silently repeat a few phrases. If one of them doesn't feel true today, you can change it or let it pass. May I be at peace. May I treat myself with patience. May I take care of myself, even on difficult days. Repeat them at your own pace, in silence.

## 06 If it's hard (silence 25 s)
These phrases may feel strange, or some emotion may come up. That's all right. Kindness toward yourself is a practice, not a feeling that has to appear right away. If something becomes too intense, feel your feet again or open your eyes. You can always come back to something simple.

## 07 Someone who cares for you (silence 40 s)
If you wish, bring to mind a person who makes you feel cared for. It can be someone close to you, or someone from your past. Notice what appears in your body as you remember them: warmth, tenderness, or nothing in particular. All of it is valid. If today you'd rather not remember anyone, you can think of a pet, a pleasant place, or simply feel the support of your hands. You don't need to bring any memory to continue.

## 08 Returning the care (silence 65 s)
If you brought someone to mind, you can offer them the same words. May you be at peace. May you be well. May you feel accompanied. If you chose a place or the support of your hands, simply stay there. Repeat the phrases silently, at your own pace.

## 09 Someone else (silence 50 s)
Now, if you'd like, think of someone you barely know: a person you pass by, someone who works nearby, or someone you saw today. That person also wants to be well, just like you. They also have good days and hard days. Silently send them the same wish: may you be at peace. May you be well.

## 10 Gratitude without obligation (silence 40 s)
Now look for something simple you can feel grateful for today. It can be very small: a warm drink, a moment of rest, this time for yourself. You don't have to feel grateful for everything, or pretend everything is fine. If today you can't find something to be grateful for, you don't need to keep looking. You can stay with the feeling of the support beneath you. That has a place in this practice too.

## 11 The warm sphere (silence 40 s)
If it helps, imagine a sphere of warm, peach-colored light near your heart. With each breath, its light can grow a little wider: first it surrounds you and, if you wish, it reaches out toward other people and toward all beings. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep feeling your hands or listening to the sounds.

## 12 Thank you (silence 90 s)
With each breath, you can silently repeat one word. As the air goes out: thank you. Thank you for this moment. Thank you for being here. If at some point you don't feel it, let it pass and return to the breath. Let it keep you company in the silence.

## 13 The heart at rest (silence 160 s)
Now let go of the phrases, the images and the words. Stay in silence, resting in whatever remains: warmth, calm, or simply stillness. You don't have to feel anything in particular. I'm going to stay in silence with you for a long while, and I'll speak to you again when it's time to return.

## 14 Integration (silence 25 s)
Little by little, feel your body again and the place where you are. If your hand is on your chest, feel its touch once more before letting it go. Notice how you are, without demanding a change. The kindness you practiced today doesn't depend on everything going well. You can offer it at any moment, starting with yourself.

## 15 Closing (silence 10 s)
Gently move your hands and feet. Stretch if you'd like, and open your eyes when you feel ready. Today, when you make a mistake, try speaking to yourself the way you would speak to someone you love. Thank you for opening your heart to this practice.
