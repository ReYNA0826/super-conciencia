# Level 5 · The Deep Mind — Witnessing the mind

## 01 Welcome (silence 8 s)
Welcome to The Deep Mind, the fifth level of Súper Conciencia. So far you have practiced arriving, settling, being present and treating yourself with kindness. Today we are going to look at the mind calmly: the thoughts, images and emotions that pass through it. This isn't about emptying the mind. It's about getting to know it a little better. As always, you can pause or stop whenever you need to.

## 02 Arriving (silence 15 s)
Settle into a steady, comfortable posture. Sitting, lying down, or however your body asks for today. If you like, close your eyes; if not, let your gaze rest low and soft. Feel the support beneath you and allow your body to rest on it.

## 03 An anchor (silence 30 s)
Before looking at the mind, choose a place to come back to when you get distracted. It can be your breath, the contact of your hands, or the support beneath your body. Choose whichever feels easiest today. Stay with it for a moment, just as it is, without changing anything.

## 04 The mind moves (silence 30 s)
Now notice that, even while you are still, the mind keeps moving. A memory appears, a plan, a worry, a song. That's what minds do. It isn't a mistake, and it doesn't mean you're doing it wrong. Just observe, for a moment, what shows up.

## 05 Naming (silence 65 s)
Let's practice something simple. When you notice a thought, give it a short name, silently and gently: memory, plan, worry, judgment, image. It doesn't matter if the name is exact. Naming it helps you notice that you are thinking, instead of getting lost inside the thought. Then come back to your anchor. Practice it now, in silence.

## 06 If you get tangled (silence 25 s)
Many times you may notice late, when you've already been inside a story for a while. That is also the practice. The moment you notice is a moment of clarity. Don't scold yourself. Just note: I was thinking. And come back.

## 07 Clouds (silence 40 s)
If it helps, imagine that thoughts are clouds crossing a wide sky. Some are light and pass quickly; others are dense and stay a little longer. You don't have to push them away or hold them back. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep naming your thoughts or feeling your hands.

## 08 Emotions in the body (silence 60 s)
Now, if you like, notice how you feel. There may be calm, sadness, impatience or joy, or perhaps a state like tiredness. If an emotion is present and it feels comfortable to explore it, notice whether a sensation in the body comes with it. You don't need to find where it is felt or understand why it appears. You can return to your hands, to the sounds, or to the support of your body whenever you want. I'll stay quiet while you choose where to rest your attention.

## 09 Noticing without responding (silence 27 s)
Thoughts appear, change and go. Some are true, some aren't. Some are useful, others are just habit. You can notice a thought without responding right away. You don't need to solve it during this practice. If it's hard to observe, come back to the contact of your hands or to the sounds around you.

## 10 Back to something simple (silence 65 s)
For a moment, stop naming what you think. Feel your hands or listen to the sounds around you. Thoughts can keep going while you attend to something simple. You don't need to wait for the mind to go quiet. I'll stay quiet while you practice.

## 11 The violet sphere (silence 35 s)
If it helps, imagine a sphere of calm violet light, floating a little above your head. You can imagine that light as a symbol of curiosity and kindness toward what you think. It doesn't need to change anything. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep listening to the sounds or feeling the support.

## 12 Observing (silence 80 s)
With each thought that appears, you can silently repeat one word: observing. Just that. Observing. Without analyzing, without arguing with the mind. I let whatever appears show itself and go. I'll stay quiet for a moment while you practice.

## 13a Rest (1) (silence 65 s)
Now let go of the word. Let go of the image too. Stay with whatever feels simplest. You can keep your attention open or rest on your anchor. If you get lost, come back without hurry. I'll stay in silence with you for a while.

## 13b Rest (2) (silence 65 s)
If it helps, notice the support beneath you once more. You can continue like this, without changing anything. I'll speak to you again when it's time to come back.

## 14 Integration (silence 25 s)
Little by little, feel your whole body again and the place where you are. Notice how your mind is now, without demanding that it be any particular way. Maybe it's calmer; maybe it's still busy. Either way, you have spent this time getting to know it a little better.

## 15 Closing (silence 10 s)
Gently move your hands and feet. Stretch if you like, and open your eyes when you feel ready. During your day, when a thought pulls at you strongly, remember that you can name it and choose what to do with it. Thank you for giving yourself this time.

# Level 6 · Expanded Awareness — Openness and expansion

## 01 Welcome (silence 8 s)
Welcome to Expanded Awareness, the sixth level of Súper Conciencia. In the previous level you observed the mind. Here, expansion means exploring a wider attention: noticing your body, the sounds and the place where you are, while you stay in touch with your surroundings. We'll open it little by little. There is no experience you have to reach. As always, you can pause or stop whenever you need to.

## 02 Arriving (silence 15 s)
Settle into a posture you can stay in for a while without effort. If you like, close your eyes; if not, let your gaze rest low and soft. Feel the support beneath you and allow your body to settle into it.

## 03 Your center (silence 30 s)
Begin with something close. Feel your breath just as it is, or the contact of your hands. This will be your center during the practice. If at any point opening up feels like too much, you can come back here whenever you want.

## 04 The whole body (silence 35 s)
Now let your attention open to the whole body, as one. Not part by part, but all at once: the weight, the temperature, the quiet areas and the ones that move. You may notice the body as a space full of small sensations. Just observe it.

## 05 The space around you (silence 65 s)
Now open your attention a little more, to the space around your body. The air on your skin, the distance to the walls, the light or darkness of the room. Listen to the sounds nearby and the ones farther away. Let your attention be wide, like a room with the windows open. I'll stay quiet while you explore.

## 06 If you feel scattered (silence 25 s)
As your attention opens, you may feel a little scattered, or the mind may wander more easily. That's normal. When it happens, come back to your center, to the breath or the hands, and from there open again, little by little. Going and coming back is also part of the practice.

## 07 The sky (silence 40 s)
If it helps, imagine the sky above the place where you are. Very wide, without edges. Clouds, sounds, thoughts and sensations can pass through it, and the sky is still the sky. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep listening to the sounds or feeling the support.

## 08 Noticing what appears (silence 65 s)
Now don't choose anything in particular. You may notice a sound, a sensation, a thought or an emotion. Each thing appears, stays for a moment and goes. You can notice what appears without having to attend to everything at once. If something feels too intense, choose a sound around you, feel the support beneath you, or open your eyes. You choose how much to explore. Practice it in silence.

## 09 Noticing (silence 30 s)
For a moment, recognize something simple: you can notice that you are listening, feeling or thinking. You don't need to find who is observing or answer any question. You can stay here, feeling the support of your body and listening to what is around you.

## 10 Connection (silence 46 s)
If it feels right, consider that, at this very moment, many other people are also breathing, feeling and seeking peace, just like you. You can silently wish them: may you be well. And wish the same for yourself. If it doesn't come naturally today, there's no need. You can keep resting in the wideness of the space or in the support of your body.

## 11 The silver sphere (silence 40 s)
If it helps, imagine a soft sphere of silver light in the center of your chest. Each time the air goes out, its light opens a little beyond your body, toward the space around you, without effort and only as far as feels pleasant. You don't need to see the image clearly. If imagining it doesn't help, you can keep feeling your hands or listening to the sounds.

## 12 Openness (silence 80 s)
With each breath, you can silently repeat one word. As the air goes out: openness. Just that. Openness. Not as something to achieve, but as an invitation to make room. I'll stay quiet for a moment while you practice.

## 13a Rest (1) (silence 68 s)
Now let go of the word. Let go of the image. Stay with whatever feels simplest. You can keep your attention open or rest in your center. If you get lost, come back without hurry. I'll stay in silence with you for a while.

## 13b Rest (2) (silence 68 s)
If it helps, notice the support beneath you once more. You can continue like this, without changing anything. I'll speak to you again when it's time to come back.

## 14 Integration (silence 25 s)
Little by little, let your attention return to your center: the breath, the hands, the support. Feel your whole body and the place where you are. Notice how you feel now, without demanding a change. Whatever you experienced, or didn't, is all right.

## 15 Closing (silence 10 s)
Gently move your hands and feet. Stretch if you like, and open your eyes when you feel ready. During your day, if you feel closed in by a problem, remember the sky: there is room for what you are living. Thank you for giving yourself this time.
