# Level 1 · Awakening — narrated script (English)

Translation of `nivel-1-el-despertar.md`. Same 15 parts, same order and the same silence after each one.
Only the text under each heading is read aloud.

## 01 Welcome (silence 8 s)
Welcome to Awakening, the first level of Súper Conciencia. For the next fifteen minutes we are going to practice something very simple: pausing... breathing... and being here. You don't need any experience. There is no right way to do this. This practice is yours, and you can pause it or end it whenever you need to. Today you don't have to solve everything. Just be here.

## 02 Posture (silence 15 s)
Find a comfortable posture. You can sit on a chair, on the floor, or lie down. Let your back lengthen gently, without stiffness. Rest your hands wherever they feel most at ease. If it feels right, close your eyes. If you prefer to keep them open, let your gaze rest on a point in front of you, without effort. Take a moment to settle in.

## 03 The place (silence 15 s)
Before turning inward, notice the place where you are. Feel the points where your body touches the chair, the bed or the floor. Notice the temperature of the air on your skin. Listen to the sounds around you, near and far, without naming them. Nothing needs to change. This place, just as it is, holds you in this moment.

## 04 Releasing the body (silence 20 s)
Now move through your body calmly. Soften your forehead... soften your eyes... release your jaw. Let your shoulders drop, away from your ears. Let your arms feel heavy. Soften your belly. Release your legs and your feet. You don't have to force yourself to relax. Simply stop holding what no longer needs to be held.

## 05 The natural breath (silence 25 s)
Bring your attention to your breathing. Don't change it. Don't make it slower or deeper. Just watch it, the way you would watch waves arriving at the shore. The air comes in... and the air goes out. Your body knows how to breathe on its own. You are only keeping it company, with curiosity, breath by breath.

## 06 Where you feel it (silence 25 s)
Find the place where you feel your breath most clearly. Perhaps at the nose, where the air comes in cool and leaves warm. Perhaps in the chest, as it opens and settles. Perhaps in the belly, as it rises and falls. Choose one of those places and let your attention rest there, gently, like a hand resting on water.

## 07 Counting (silence 45 s)
If it helps, you can count your breaths. As you breathe out, count one. On the next breath, two. Continue like that until you reach ten, and then begin again. If you lose count, that's all right. Simply return to one. I will stay silent for a moment, so you can practice at your own pace.

## 08 The mind wanders (silence 20 s)
You may have noticed it already: the mind wanders. A memory appears, a task, a worry. That is not a mistake. It is what minds do. Meditating is not making the mind go blank. Meditating is noticing that you drifted away... and coming back. Each time you return, you are practicing. Each return is the awakening.

## 09 Naming the thought (silence 45 s)
When you notice that a thought has carried you away, you can say to yourself, kindly: thinking. Without judging yourself. Without arguing with the thought. Let it pass, like a cloud crossing the sky, and feel again the air coming in and going out. Let's practice this in silence.

## 10 The whole body (silence 25 s)
Now open your attention to your whole body. Feel how the breath moves it gently, from head to toe. If there is an area that feels tense or uncomfortable, observe it with respect. You don't have to fix it. If you need to move, do it slowly, with attention. Your body is not an obstacle to meditation. It is the place where the present happens.

## 11 The blue sphere (silence 30 s)
If you like, imagine in front of you a sphere of blue light, serene and calm. As the air comes in, the sphere gently expands. As the air goes out, it settles back. You don't need to see it clearly. It is enough to feel its rhythm, which is your own. You breathe... and the light breathes with you.

## 12 I am here (silence 50 s)
With each breath, you can silently repeat two words. As the air comes in: I am. As the air goes out: here. I am... here. Not in what happened yesterday. Not in what will come tomorrow. I am... here. Let those words stay with you during the silence.

## 13 Conscious silence (silence 75 s)
Now let go of the words as well. Let go of the counting. Let go of any effort. Stay in silence, simply being. If the mind wanders, you already know the way back: one breath. I will be here, and I will speak to you again in a moment.

## 14 Integration (silence 20 s)
Little by little, feel the weight of your body again. Listen once more to the sounds of the place. Notice how you feel now, without comparing it to how you expected to feel. Perhaps there is more calm, perhaps not. Both are fine. What matters is that you paused, you breathed, and you came back to the present, again and again.

## 15 Closing (silence 10 s)
Gently move your fingers and your toes. If you wish, stretch your body. When you feel ready, open your eyes. Remember: meditating is practicing attention, not getting rid of thoughts. You can return to this breath at any moment of your day. Thank you for giving yourself this time. Today you didn't have to solve everything. Just be here.
